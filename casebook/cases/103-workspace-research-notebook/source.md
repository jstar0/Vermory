# Research Workspace Notebook

`carbon-policy-notebook` is a non-code workspace for a policy research report.

The workspace contains PDF notes, extracted tables, and a draft outline. The current research question is whether provincial carbon-market pilots changed manufacturing energy intensity.

The latest decision is to use 2018-2024 panel data and keep firm-level causal claims out of the report unless the source pack later supports them.

The active output is a literature matrix, not a dashboard or software prototype.
