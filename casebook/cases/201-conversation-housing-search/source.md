# Conversation Housing Search

Renee is relocating to Seattle and is using chat as an ongoing apartment search assistant rather than working inside any repository.

The search started with a target move-in window around September 2026, a monthly rent ceiling of 3200 USD, and a strong preference for a one-bedroom instead of a studio.

Renee works near South Lake Union three days a week and wants a typical one-way commute under 35 minutes by transit.

She has a medium dog, so pet-friendly buildings are required.

Early in the conversation she considered Capitol Hill, Fremont, and Lower Queen Anne, but she later said Fremont became the preferred area because it balanced dog access and commute.

She also said a unit without in-unit laundry is acceptable only as a fallback, not as a first-choice filter.

One candidate was `Aurora 8`, which looked promising on commute and price but had no pet-friendly policy, so it should be excluded from future serious recommendations.

Another candidate was `Juniper Row`, which stayed in consideration after a later clarification because it accepts dogs and is still within budget.

Renee occasionally forwards apartment links from email and texts, and sometimes starts a new chat thread just to ask about one listing, but those adjacent threads should not automatically overwrite the main search unless she explicitly says to do so.
